About the World

A land held together by friendship

Equestria is a bright, sprawling land of rolling meadows, floating cloud cities, and crystal-spired castles. Three kinds of ponies share it: clever Unicorns, sky-racing Pegasi, and steady, hardworking Earth Ponies — along with dragons, griffons, and countless other magical creatures.

At the center of it all sits one simple idea: that friendship isn't just nice to have. It's the most powerful magic there is.
